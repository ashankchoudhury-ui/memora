The problem.

Your saved collections are becoming enormous libraries of forgotten information.

The saving graveyard

You save hundreds of useful things but rarely revisit them. They disappear into your Saved folder forever.

Retrieval failure

You remember what something was about but forget its title, source or location. Searching doesn't help.

Manual organization

Creating folders, adding tags and organizing everything manually takes effort you don't have time for.

Forgotten intentions

You saved something because you wanted to do something with it later, but eventually forgot why you saved it.

Information overload

You collect more information than you can realistically process. Your library grows faster than your ability to use it.

Fragmented sources

Your saved content is scattered across Instagram, YouTube, articles and random folders. No unified view.

How MEMORA fixes it.

One unified memory that captures everything, understands what matters, and retrieves it instantly.

Capture anything

Save links, images, PDFs, screenshots, notes and ideas with a single share. MEMORA accepts almost anything you encounter across the internet.

Understand everything

AI processes your saved content: summarizes it, identifies topics, connects related items and remembers what matters to you personally.

Find anything

Search using what you remember, not exact keywords. Ask your AI memory assistant and get the right result instantly.

The result is a single place where everything you've saved actually becomes useful again.
