Memory Priority

Not everything you save matters equally.

Tell MEMORA what's important to you. Your priorities shape how your memory system works.

Critical

"I must not lose this reference for the project deadline."

Important

Don't want to forget this. Regular resurfacing and relevant recommendations.

"I want to revisit this tutorial before my next sprint."

Interesting

Worth remembering. Searchable, organized and occasionally resurfaced.

"Cool concept — I may want to explore this later."

Casual

Just saving this for later. Stored without unnecessary notifications.

"Interesting, but not urgent. I'll find it when I need it."

FAQ

Common questions.

What is MEMORA? +
MEMORA is an AI-powered personal knowledge engine that helps you save, organize and retrieve information from across the internet. It understands what you save, why you save it, and helps you find it when you need it.
Is MEMORA available yet? +
MEMORA is currently in pre-development validation. We're collecting early-access signups and validating demand before building the full application. Join the waitlist to be among the first to know when early access opens.
What can I save? +
The planned product supports links, images, screenshots, PDFs, documents, notes and ideas. You'll be able to share content from Instagram, YouTube, articles, and more directly into MEMORA.
Can I save Instagram posts? +
The intended workflow allows users to share Instagram links into MEMORA. Available metadata and previews will depend on platform restrictions. We never promise unrestricted content extraction from any platform.
Is MEMORA free? +
The initial product is planned to have a free tier with basic features. Additional premium features may be introduced later. We'll share pricing details closer to launch.
How does AI help? +
AI will help categorize information, generate summaries, connect related content and retrieve saved information using natural-language queries. You won't need to manually organize every item.
